What is GDG on Campus?

Google Developer Groups on Campus are university-based communities supported by Google, bringing Google's developer ecosystem directly to students.

Rajeev Institute of Technology Hassan, Karnataka
Dept. of Computer Science & Engineering

Our Story

GDG on Campus – RIT Hassan is a student-led technology community at Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan, affiliated with Google Developer Groups. We empower students to explore modern technologies, build real projects, and connect with the global developer ecosystem.

Our community fosters innovation, creativity, and collaboration among students across all disciplines — not just computer science.

Google Supported Official Google Developer Group chapter on campus
Student-Led Run entirely by passionate student volunteers
Hands-on Learning Workshops, hackathons, and real project experience
Global Network Part of worldwide GDG developer community

Learn

Explore Google technologies through workshops, talks, and study groups led by peers and industry experts.

Build

Apply knowledge by developing real projects, participating in hackathons, and creating solutions that matter.

Share knowledge, experiences, and projects with the community. Mentor others and grow together.

Grow

Build professional skills, networks, and confidence for a successful career in technology.
